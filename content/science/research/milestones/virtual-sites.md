## Overview

Development of a virtual site force field prototype to better capture anisotropic charge distributions — particularly sigma holes on halogens and lone pairs on nitrogen.

## Scientific goals

- SMIRNOFF-native virtual site typing via SMARTS-based chemical perception
- Automated fitting of virtual site positions and charges against QM electrostatic potential surfaces
- Improved performance on FF benchmarks
