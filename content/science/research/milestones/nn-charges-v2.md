## Overview

Second-generation neural network charge model fit directly to QM properties such as electric field, generated at a high level of theory. This will explicitly incorporate solvent effects.

## Scientific goals

- Extended training set covering a broader range of drug-relevant chemical space
- Improved performance on real-world benchmarking tasks such as solvation free energies
- Integration with the OpenFF Toolkit
