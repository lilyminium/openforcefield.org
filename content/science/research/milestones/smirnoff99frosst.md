## Overview

smirnoff99Frosst was the first force field in the SMIRNOFF format — a minimalist AMBER-compatible general small molecule force field developed at Merck Frosst using direct chemical perception. It achieved comparable accuracy to GAFF with a dramatic reduction in parameter count: from over 6,000 lines in GAFF to approximately 300 lines, made possible by SMARTS-based typing.

smirnoff99Frosst served as the starting point for Parsley and demonstrated that direct chemical perception could produce competitive general force fields.

**This repository is no longer maintained, as of 2026.**
