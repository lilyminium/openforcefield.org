## Overview

Alpha release of Rosemary (OpenFF 3.x), the third-generation OpenFF small molecule force field. The Rosemary alpha force field is a **work in progress** force field with parameters co-optimized to peptide and small molecule data. It uses the AshGC charge model.

This alpha release is intended for community testing and feedback ahead of a stable release. Users are encouraged to benchmark Rosemary against their use cases and report issues.
