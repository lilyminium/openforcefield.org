## Overview

Parsley (OpenFF 1.y.z) was the first generation of optimised OpenFF small molecule force fields. Starting from smirnoff99Frosst, parameters were refit using ForceBalance against large sets of quantum chemical data — representing a substantial improvement over the hand-tuned starting point.

Parsley introduced systematic parameter optimisation for the OpenFF Initiative and demonstrated that data-driven methods could produce high-quality general force fields competitive with GAFF and CGenFF.

Minor releases (1.1.0 through 1.3.1) continued to expand QM training datasets and fix specific chemical perception issues.
