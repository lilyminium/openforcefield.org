## Overview

AshGC is a deep learning partial charge model for fast, high-quality partial charge assignment. It provides AM1-BCC quality charges at a fraction of the computational cost, enabling rapid charge assignment for large compound libraries and high-throughput screening workflows.

The model is the first-generation OpenFF neural network charge model and serves as the foundation for [Neural network charges v2](/science/research/milestones/nn-charges-v2/).
