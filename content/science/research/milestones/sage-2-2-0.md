## Overview

Updates to Sage 2.1.0 including improvements to small ring angles and sulfamide parameters. Contributions from Alexandra McIsaac and Pavan Behara, with benchmarking infrastructure from Matthew Thompson, Jeffrey Wagner, and Lily Wang.

## Changes

- Improved small ring angle parameters
- Improved sulfamide parameters
- Benchmarking results across a broad set of drug-like molecules
