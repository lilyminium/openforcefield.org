## Overview

Sage 2.1.0 improved chemical perception for sulfonamides and phosphates, extended training set coverage to include Gen2 datasets alongside Gen1 data, and refined fitting procedures using a physically intuitive starting point from Modified Seminario — also including dihedral deviations in optimised geometry targets and fitting impropers.

## Changes

- Improved SMARTS typing for sulfonamides and phosphates
- Improved bridgehead nitrogen and carbon parameters
- Improved parameters for groups with delocalised charges
- Physically motivated starting point from Modified Seminario for bonds and angles
- Data-driven improper torsion values
- Broader training target coverage
