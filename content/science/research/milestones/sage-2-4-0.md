## Overview

The next minor Sage release. This force field will be fit using a Pytorch-based framework, based on the [smee](https://github.com/openforcefield/smee) and [descent](https://github.com/openforcefield/descent) libraries.

## Scientific goals

- Extended QM training datasets covering additional chemical diversity
- Continued improvements to torsion accuracy
- Refinements to Lennard-Jones parameters using condensed-phase benchmarks
