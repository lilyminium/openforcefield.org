## Overview

Co-optimization of a 3-site water model alongside OpenFF small molecule force field parameters to both pure and mixture properties.

## Scientific goals

- Joint optimisation of water and non-water non-bonded parameters
- Validated against physical property data, experimental solvation free energies, and protein–ligand benchmarks
- Delivers equivalent or improved performance to existing water models with OpenFF parameters (e.g. TIP3P, OPC3)
