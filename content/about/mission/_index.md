*The Open Force Field Initiative's mission is to develop the next generation of accurate, transferable, and openly available force fields for molecular simulation — built through open science, open data, and open-source software.*

# Open Force Field Initiative

The **Open Force Field Initiative** is a network of academic and industry researchers working together to advance the science and infrastructure required for the next generation of small molecule and biomolecular force fields. Our goal is to develop automated, systematic, data-driven techniques to parameterize and assess more accurate force fields, released under open licenses for the community to use, validate, and extend.

{{< br >}}

# Open Force Field Consortium

The **Open Force Field Consortium** is a pre-competitive, industry-funded collaboration to build more accurate small molecule force fields and improve predictive power in computational drug discovery.

Consortium members actively shape the work. Industry partners bring real-world expertise in force field strengths and weaknesses, and the Initiative benefits directly from that experience. Partners contributing above a threshold level join the **Consortium Advisory Board (CAB)**, which provides input on scientific directions and project plans. The **Governing Board** — comprising two elected industry representatives and five Principal Investigators — makes strategic decisions and oversees expenditures.
