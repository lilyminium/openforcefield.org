Force fields released by the Open Force Field Initiative are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/openforcefield/openff-forcefields/releases) in [SMIRNOFF format](https://openforcefield.github.io/standards/standards/smirnoff/). Details about this format are in our [publication](https://www.doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00640). Usage examples are in our [documentation](https://docs.openforcefield.org/en/latest/examples.html).

For issues, email [support@openforcefield.org](mailto:support@openforcefield.org) or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/openforcefield/openff-forcefields/issues).
